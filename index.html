Buyers are looking for Indian suppliers. Most can't find them.

Demand is moving.

Manufacturers in Europe, the UK and the US are actively adding suppliers outside China. The India-UK trade agreement, in force since July 2026, removed duties on most Indian goods, and Europe has been closing chemical and industrial capacity, pushing buyers to look abroad.

Supply is invisible.

India has thousands of capable mid-sized manufacturers, but most have no export sales team, thin websites and no way to reach the right buyer in Germany or the UK. Buyers can't find them, and they can't find buyers.

Traditional help doesn't scale.

Trade fairs are expensive and occasional, agents take large commissions, and agencies sell activity, not results. Researching markets and reaching thousands of qualified buyers by hand needs a team most manufacturers will never hire.

One AI engine for export growth

We built a set of AI agents that do the work of an export sales department: research, buyer discovery, outreach and follow-through, with people reviewing every important decision.

Market radar

Agents study trade data, competitors and buyer requirements for each product every day, and rank the countries and customer types with the strongest demand for what a manufacturer makes.

Buyer finder

Agents build verified lists of companies that import the relevant products, sorted by customer type, with the people to contact and the evidence behind each name.

Outreach studio

Every customer type gets its own message, written for its needs and grounded in verified research, sent through dedicated email infrastructure and reviewed by our team before it goes out.

Lead desk

Every reply and enquiry is logged in one shared register with its source, passed to the manufacturer quickly, and tracked through presentations, costings and quotations to an order.

From product to purchase order

  1. Understand the product. We start with what the manufacturer makes, its certifications and its capacity.
  2. Map the market. Market radar ranks the countries and customer types where demand and fit are strongest.
  3. Find the buyers. Buyer finder builds a list of real companies with the people to contact.
  4. Reach out. Outreach studio runs tailored campaigns for each customer type.
  5. Convert. Qualified enquiries go to the manufacturer, and we help with presentations, costings and quotations.
  6. Learn and adjust. Every reply feeds back into the research, so targeting improves every week.

Two ways we work

For Indian manufacturers: export growth

We run the engine for manufacturers who want new export customers. The model is performance-based: a small monthly fee covers direct running costs, and we earn mainly through a share of sales to the new customers we bring in. If we don't deliver customers, we don't earn.

For overseas buyers: precision components from India

For buyers who want one accountable partner in India, we supply precision components made to drawing and inspected before they ship: busbars, lugs and terminals, flexible connectors, stamped and turned parts, contacts and coils, welded subassemblies, and brass fittings. Our research engine finds the best-matched manufacturers; we manage quality and delivery.

Questions, answered

Who do you work with?

Indian manufacturers that want export customers, and overseas companies that want reliable suppliers in India.

Which countries do you target?

We start with the UK, Germany and the Benelux, and the Gulf, and expand where research shows strong demand.

How do you charge manufacturers?

A small monthly fee covers direct running costs such as data and email infrastructure. We earn mainly through a share of sales to new customers we bring in.

Do you replace a manufacturer's sales team?

No. We find and qualify the buyers and help with presentations and quotations; the manufacturer approves every quotation and owns the customer relationship.

How do you use AI?

Agents research markets, find and verify buyers, and draft tailored outreach. Our team reviews conclusions and messages before they are used.

Is my data safe?

Business information shared with us stays confidential and is used only for that partner's work.
